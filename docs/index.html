Validation, uncertainty and limitations
  • Disease presence is the UCI angiographic status field, with codes 1–4 mapped to positive and 0 to negative. The task concerns disease already present at the time of assessment.
  • The 70/30 Cleveland split is stratified by outcome. Median and mode imputation, scaling and fixed-code one-hot encoding are inside the Python pipeline. Learned preprocessing and model fitting use the 70% partition only. Both model specifications are fixed and the Budapest set is used for evaluation.
  • Ten available inputs include symptoms, resting measurements and exercise-test measurements. Exercise ST slope and thallium are omitted for extensive Budapest missingness. Fluoroscopy is omitted because it is near the angiographic assessment and is rarely recorded in Budapest.
  • 95% percentile intervals use 2,000 bootstrap resamples, stratified by outcome within each evaluation cohort. The difference interval subtracts independently resampled Cleveland AUROC from Budapest AUROC. Intervals describe uncertainty conditional on the fixed fitted models.
  • The cohorts are small, historical and selected for angiographic evaluation. Differences can reflect case mix or collection practices. The results are exploratory and intended for research demonstration.
